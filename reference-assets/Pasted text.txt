SAXENA SKIN AND COSMETIC CLINIC

Complete Website Development, Branding & Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) Brief

Location: Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, India

Project objective: To develop a premium, modern, medically credible and highly SEO-optimised website that establishes Saxena Skin and Cosmetic Clinic as a leading multidisciplinary centre for dermatology, hair transplantation, laser treatments, cosmetic dermatology, gynaecology and facial aesthetics in Jabalpur and the surrounding region.

1. OUR VISION

We do not want a basic clinic website that simply displays our address, doctors and contact details.

We want to develop a comprehensive digital platform that:

1. Establishes Saxena Skin and Cosmetic Clinic as a leading specialist medical centre in Jabalpur.
2. Achieves strong organic Google visibility for dermatology, skin specialists, hair transplantation, laser treatments and cosmetic procedures.
3. Builds credibility and trust through the qualifications, expertise and experience of our five consultants.
4. Generates new patient enquiries and appointments.
5. Educates patients through high-quality, medically reviewed content.
6. Establishes a premium brand identity comparable to leading specialist clinics in metropolitan cities.
7. Develops long-term digital authority for the clinic.

The website must be designed with SEO as a foundation rather than treating SEO as something to add after development.

Our ultimate goal is to make Saxena Skin and Cosmetic Clinic one of the first and most credible options people discover when searching for relevant specialist medical services in Jabalpur.

15. TECHNICAL SEO

The website must have:

- Mobile-first responsive design.
- Fast loading speed.
- HTTPS and SSL.
- XML sitemap.
- Proper robots.txt.
- Correct indexing settings.
- Canonical URLs.
- Structured data and schema markup.
- Google Analytics 4.
- Google Search Console.
- Google Maps integration.
- Image compression using WebP/AVIF where supported.
- Good Core Web Vitals.
- Accessible navigation and readable typography.
- Regular backups and security updates.

Important schema types should include LocalBusiness or the most appropriate MedicalBusiness subtype, Physician for individual doctors, and relevant structured data for articles and website navigation.

All structured data must accurately reflect visible, verified website information.
